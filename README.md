## 📊 Google Play Store Analysis
#### 📁 Project Overview
This project analyzes app data from the Google Play Store to uncover trends, user behavior, and performance across various app types, genres, and pricing strategies.

## ✅ Objectives
Identify the most expensive and highest-rated apps

Compare app performance by genre, price, and rating

Analyze user sentiment from app reviews

Understand install trends and Android compatibility

Offer actionable insights and recommendations

## 🧩 Dataset
The dataset includes information such as:

App Name, Category, Rating

Size, Price, Reviews, Installs

Content Rating, Android Version

Sentiment Polarity & Subjectivity (from reviews)

## 📈 Tools Used
Excel (for data cleaning and exploration)

## 🔍 Key Insights
“I am Rich” and its editions are the most expensive apps

“Tools” is the most common app genre

Paid apps have a slightly higher average rating than free apps

Over 64% of user reviews are positive

Most apps support Android 4.0+

2018 had the highest number of apps, ratings, and prices

other

## 💡 Recommendations
Focus on mid-range pricing for paid apps

Optimize app size for better downloads

Improve features based on neutral/negative feedback

Target “Everyone” content rating for maximum reach

Use seasonal timing for app updates or launches
